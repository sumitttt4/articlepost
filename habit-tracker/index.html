How to use it

  1. Download the Google Sheets appInstall it from the Play Store or App Store and sign in with your Google account.
  2. Open either habit tracker linkTap Preview on the tracker you like above.
  3. Make a copy and use your copyTap Make a copy, or in the sheet choose File, then Make a copy. Track your habits in the copied version, not the original.

Always work in your own copy. The original stays untouched so the next person can copy it too.
